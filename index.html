Education Cascade — from primary school funding to consequences

This cascade traces how underfunding of Danish primary school (folkeskolen) is argued to ripple through learning, the labour market, income, health, and inequality. Each arrow is a cited causal claim from the literature — direction, time lag, strength, and confidence — not a model prediction. Hover an arrow for its source. The point is to make the argument testable: where does it hold up, where does it break, and what data would we need to know for sure.

The green transition / just transition

GHG↓ → short-term sector unemployment (coal/oil/transport) → offset by green jobs over time → better air quality → more healthy life years. The classic trade-off with negative short-term, positive long-term.

Data: DST DRIVHUS, ENE2HO, green GVA · IPCC AR6 WG3 (just-transition costs), GBD (air quality→HALE).
Illustrative

Inequality and social cohesion

Income distribution (Gini) → social trust → institutional trust → life satisfaction. The soft-data cascade — where the data wishlist is longest, because trust measures aren't produced in Denmark today.

Data: Gini (DST), but trust is missing → World Values Survey / Eurobarometer / EU-SILC.
Illustrative

The care economy / invisible work

Unpaid care, housework, volunteering → wellbeing, health, women's labour market participation. The GDP-invisible cascade — almost pure data wishlist, which is the point.

Data: time-use surveys (sparse) · satellite accounts for household production.
